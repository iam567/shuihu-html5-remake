Use case: game UI component sheet. Style: classic Chinese scroll-and-rosewood interface for a Song-dynasty strategy RPG. Dark polished rosewood (紫檀) frames with fine gold inlay lines, small red-lacquer and gold corner fittings, warm cream xuan-paper surfaces with a faint fibre texture, crisp clean edges, soft even lighting, no jade green. Draw separate components on a perfectly flat pure magenta #ff00ff background with wide magenta gaps and nothing else (no shadows on the background, no text anywhere). Left half: (1) a portrait frame, a tall rounded-rectangle rosewood frame with gold inlay and a small red silk tassel hanging from the bottom centre, about 2:3 tall; its whole inner opening is flat pure magenta #ff00ff (empty). Right half, top to bottom: (2) a wide name plaque, rosewood border with gold corner fittings around an empty cream paper centre, about 5:1; (3) a long horizontal stat bar, thin rosewood frame with gold line and an empty cream paper centre, about 7:1; (4) a row of three identical square equipment slots, rosewood frame with gold corners around a slightly darker inset paper well.
