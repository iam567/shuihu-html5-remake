Use case: seamless retouch of a game map. Edit target: the supplied top-down map of a Chinese strategy RPG (Water Margin, Song dynasty) was assembled from separately painted square tiles, so it shows tile seams: grass, dirt, water and floor patches that do not continue across cell edges, roofs, trees and walls cut off or doubled at cell borders, colour jumps between neighbouring cells. Repaint it as ONE seamless, coherent picture in HD-2D style: detailed pixel-art textures lit like a 3D diorama, soft ambient occlusion, gentle bloom and warm sunlight: continuous ground textures and lighting, complete roofs and tree crowns, clean outlines. Strict geometry lock: the output must line up pixel for pixel with the reference when overlaid. Keep every house, wall, gate, door, fence, tree, rock, barrel, crate, well, table and piece of furniture at exactly the same position and size, same footprint, same outline; do not add, remove, move, resize or rotate anything; keep roads, paths, rivers and shorelines on exactly the same course and width. Pure black areas are empty space outside the map: keep them pure black #000000. No text, letters or signs with writing, no characters or people, no frame or border.
